# Lab Sheet 02 – LLMs in Everyday Life

## 1. Introduction

This practical activity focuses on exploring how Large Language Models (LLMs) can be used to address everyday challenges through creative problem-solving and product development.

The activity involves using an LLM as a product-development partner to identify problems, generate innovative ideas, develop product concepts, and design potential solutions for real-world situations.

Our group was assigned **Group 5 – The Busy Student**, which focuses on the time-management challenges faced by university students.

## 2. Problem Description – The Busy Student

University students have to manage several responsibilities simultaneously, including lectures, assignments, group projects, examinations, extracurricular activities, personal commitments, and deadlines.

Balancing these activities can become difficult, especially when students have multiple tasks to complete within limited time periods. Important activities may be forgotten, deadlines may be missed, and poor time management can affect academic performance and personal well-being.

The challenge was to develop an innovative product that helps university students organize their activities, manage their schedules, and use their time more effectively.

## 3. Product Development Challenge

The main objective of this activity was to use an LLM as a product-development partner to create a solution for the identified time-management problem.

The required product development components included:

- **Product Concept:** Develop an innovative solution addressing the time-management challenges of university students.
- **Product Name:** Create a suitable and meaningful name for the proposed product.
- **Key Features:** Identify the main functionalities that would help students organize and manage their daily activities.
- **Target Users:** Identify the intended users and their specific needs.
- **Visual Prototype/Mock-up:** Develop a visual representation demonstrating the proposed product's interface and functionality.

## 4. Use of Large Language Models

Large Language Models were used as supporting tools throughout the product-development process.

The LLM assisted with generating product ideas, exploring possible solutions, identifying useful features, defining target users, and improving the overall product concept.

It also helped structure the proposed solution and support the development of a visual prototype.

The generated suggestions were reviewed and considered during the development process to ensure that the final concept addressed the identified problem.

## 5. What We Gained

- Learned how to use Large Language Models as product-development partners.
- Improved creative thinking and problem-solving skills by addressing a real-world university student challenge.
- Gained experience in identifying user problems and developing suitable product concepts.
- Learned how to define a product's name, key features, and target users.
- Gained practical experience in designing visual prototypes and mock-ups.
- Understood how AI can support the development of innovative solutions for improving students' time management and productivity.
- Improved our ability to communicate ideas and collaborate as a group.

## 6. Conclusion

This practical activity provided an opportunity to explore the application of Large Language Models beyond traditional text generation. By focusing on the challenges faced by busy university students, we experienced how LLMs can support the process of identifying problems, generating ideas, and developing innovative product solutions.

Overall, the activity demonstrated the potential of generative AI as a useful tool for creative thinking, product development, and solving everyday challenges.
